Architectural Brilliance Meets Serene Living

The Oura Residence stands as a testament to uncompromising design and meticulous craftsmanship. Set against a backdrop of sweeping panoramas, this property redefines modern luxury with its expansive open-plan living spaces, floor-to-ceiling glazing, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow.

Every material has been carefully curated to evoke a sense of quiet opulence—from the warm, hand-scraped hardwood floors to the custom-milled cabinetry and imported stone surfaces. The residence is designed not just for shelter, but as a holistic environment that nurtures calm and sophisticated living.

The heart of the home is a chef-grade kitchen that flows effortlessly into formal and informal dining areas, all oriented towards the spectacular vistas and the meticulously landscaped grounds. It is an estate built for those who appreciate the luxury of space and the subtlety of truly exceptional design.
